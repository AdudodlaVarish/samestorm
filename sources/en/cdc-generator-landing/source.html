Generator Safety Fact Sheet

For Everyone

Purpose

  • Portable back-up generators produce the poison gas carbon monoxide (CO). CO is an odorless, colorless gas that kills without warning.
  • Learn quick facts about carbon monoxide poisoning from generator use and how to prevent it.
  • This fact sheet is available in many languages.

Carbon Monoxide Poisoning 

Carbon monoxide (CO) is an odorless, colorless gas produced when fossil fuel is burnt. CO poisoning can cause sudden illness and death, but it can be prevented.
