Prevention Guidelines:
You Can Prevent Carbon Monoxide Exposure


    z   DO have your heating system, water heater and any other
        gas, oil, or coal burning appliances serviced by a qualified
        technician every year.
    z   DO install a battery-operated CO detector in your home and
        check or replace the battery when you change the time on
        your clocks each spring and fall. If the detector sounds,
        leave your home immediately and call 911.

    z   DO seek prompt medical attention if you suspect CO
        poisoning and are feeling dizzy, light-headed, or nauseous.
    z   DON’T use a generator, charcoal grill, camp stove, or other
        gasoline or charcoal-burning device inside your home,
        basement, or garage or near a window.

    z   DON’T run a car or truck inside a garage attached to your
        house, even if you leave the door open.
    z   DON’T burn anything in a stove or fireplace that isn’t vented.

    z   DON’T heat your house with a gas oven.
    z   DON’T use a generator, pressure washer, or any
        gasoline-powered engine less than 20 feet from any
        window, door, or vent. Use an extension cord that is more
        than 20 feet long to keep the generator at a safe distance.
